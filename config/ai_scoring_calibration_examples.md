Title: Senior Product Manager — Payments Platform
Reason pattern: Positives — clear end-to-end ownership of payment workflows, roadmap and stakeholder management, fintech domain match. Penalties — none significant. Overall — strong fit; candidate background aligns closely with platform PM in payments.

### Example B — Senior PM, 7–8 years, B2B SaaS (score 5–7)
Title: Senior Product Manager — Developer Tools
Reason pattern: Positives — solid PM ownership, roadmap, cross-functional delivery in B2B SaaS. Penalties — asks for 7–8 years (mild seniority stretch), domain is developer tools rather than fintech. Overall — partial fit worth reviewing; seniority and domain are mild negatives, not disqualifiers.

### Example C — Pharma brand-marketing PM (score 0–2)
Title: Product Manager — Oncology Marketing
Reason pattern: Positives — none significant; role is brand/marketing in pharmaceuticals, not technology product ownership. Penalties — no PM ownership, wrong function and industry; product skill transfer is weak. Overall — poor fit; fundamentally outside target PM profile.
